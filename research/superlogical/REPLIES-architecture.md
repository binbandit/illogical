# Architecture, parking, remote, and CLI reply research

Read on 20 September 2026 using the signed-in X interface in dedicated Chrome research tabs. This is a bounded follow-up to the video inventory: developer replies were read together with their parent questions, relevant nested conversations were opened, and truncated developer answers were expanded. No posts, replies, likes, follows, or account settings were changed.

The six root conversations were [V02 architecture](https://x.com/mitchellh/status/2082936029426892960), [V09 basic persistence](https://x.com/mitchellh/status/2093451043661316217), [V11 parking](https://x.com/mitchellh/status/2095232081853039041), [V12 remote sessions](https://x.com/mitchellh/status/2097424868203758046), [V14 CLI](https://x.com/mitchellh/status/2099622049325232505), and [V15 CLI game](https://x.com/mitchellh/status/2099915915056074807). X displayed 149, 402, 41, 147, 122, and 43 replies respectively during the review; these counts include nested replies and do not mean every reply was inspected. The relevant ordering and the loaded threads were examined, with targeted expansion of substantive follow-ups. This is not an exhaustive archive of all deleted, hidden, or lower-ranked replies.

All claims below are paraphrases. A developer describing a prototype establishes a stated capability at that time, not a shipped release guarantee. A proposed mechanism remains a proposal. A community answer does not become a developer promise.

| RA04 | [Jonathan Slenders](https://x.com/jonathan_s/status/2083628025300623817) asks whether the server must parse terminal escapes too, despite the performance explanation. | [Mitchell](https://x.com/mitchellh/status/2083630166191043007) confirms server parsing, but says bytes are split toward clients ahead of that parsing so both can parse concurrently; queues accommodate slow participants. | Correct any claim that Superlogical eliminates the second parse. ## Recommended inventory changes

1. Add opt-in synchronized viewports as a confirmed developer-stated collaboration capability, with sharing/permission UX still unknown.
2. Add queued PTY catch-up after brief disconnection and explicit resynchronization signaling when retention is exhausted. Keep numeric limits and input-delivery semantics unspecified.
3. Record that an in-browser client was stated to work in an early prototype. Preserve the distinction between that statement and an observed/shipped browser feature matrix.
4. Mark terminal capability-bitset negotiation as a proposal. Do not count it as verified implementation behavior.
5. Retain newer QUIC/fallback evidence while dating the earlier HTTP/WebSocket explanation. Do not combine all revisions into an invented current transport stack.
6. Preserve the unanswered questions above as research gaps, not implementation requirements implied by community enthusiasm.
7. Add the confirmed NixOS deployment module as R11, missing in illogical, and existing multiplayer capability as R12, only partially represented by our multi-client same-user service.
8. Record Kubernetes, floating-panel and notification-inbox directions as plans/design work. Keep the older resize policy dated and distinguish it from the proposed replacement and later owner metadata.

Only this research note was changed in this pass. Runtime code, the existing feature inventory, and the service audit were left unchanged for the coordinating agent to integrate deliberately.
